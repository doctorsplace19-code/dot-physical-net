DOT Consortium FAQs

What is a DOT random testing consortium (C/TPA)?

A consortium / third-party administrator (C/TPA) pools drivers into a random drug and alcohol testing program and manages random selections, MRO review, and FMCSA Clearinghouse reporting so you stay DOT-compliant.

How much does the consortium cost?

Enrollment starts at $49 per year per driver (Basic plan). That covers random pool enrollment, MRO services, and required compliance reporting. The Premium all-inclusive plan starts at $220/driver/first year.

Do owner-operators need to join a consortium?

Yes. FMCSA requires any driver operating a commercial motor vehicle under their own authority to be enrolled in a random drug and alcohol testing consortium. Solo owner-operators cannot self-administer random testing.

What is the DOT random testing rate for 2026?

The FMCSA minimum annual random testing rates are 50% for drugs and 10% for alcohol, based on the average number of driver positions in the pool.

What happens if a driver fails a random test?

The driver is immediately removed from safety-sensitive duties and must complete the DOT return-to-duty (RTD) process with a Substance Abuse Professional (SAP) before returning to driving.

Does the consortium report to the FMCSA Clearinghouse?

Yes. We handle the required FMCSA Clearinghouse reporting and queries as part of the consortium program.

How fast can I get enrolled?

Enrollment is same-day online. You receive your consortium enrollment documentation right away.
