DOT Physical Cost FAQ

Does insurance cover DOT physicals?

Generally no. DOT physicals are a federal regulatory requirement, not a medical treatment, and most health plans do not cover them. They are an out-of-pocket expense — deductible as a business expense for self-employed drivers and employers.

How often do I need to pay for a DOT physical?

Every 24 months for most drivers. If your examiner issues a shorter certificate (12 months, for example, due to controlled hypertension or sleep apnea), you pay for a renewal exam at that frequency.

Who pays for the DOT physical — me or my employer?

Either can pay. Many employers cover DOT physicals as an employment cost. Owner-operators and self-employed CDL drivers pay out of pocket. DotPhysical.net supports both: individuals can pay directly, and employers can set up an account to pay for their fleet.

Can I get a DOT physical and drug test at the same location?

Yes. Most DotPhysical.net locations offer both at the same visit. Order your DOT physical ($110) and drug test ($80) together and handle both in one trip.

Are DOT physical costs tax deductible?

For self-employed owner-operators, yes — DOT physicals are generally deductible as a business expense on Schedule C. Consult a tax professional for your specific situation.
