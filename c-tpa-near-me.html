What Is a C/TPA?

A C/TPA (Consortium/Third-Party Administrator) is an organization authorized under 49 CFR Part 40 to manage DOT drug and alcohol testing programs on behalf of employers. The C/TPA handles the administrative requirements so drivers and carriers can stay compliant without managing the program themselves.

A C/TPA does not need to be physically near you — all services are managed electronically. Testing happens at collection sites near you.

Your C/TPA is responsible for:

🎲

Random Pool Management

Maintains the pool of enrolled drivers and conducts scientifically valid random selection draws

📬

Selection Notifications

Notifies you when selected and provides collection site instructions with required timeframes

🔬

MRO Coordination

Works with a Medical Review Officer to verify all test results before reporting

📋

Clearinghouse Reporting

Reports all violations, refusals, and RTD completions to the FMCSA Drug & Alcohol Clearinghouse

📊

Compliance Records

Maintains required testing records for FMCSA audits and DOT inspections

🔄

Follow-Up Testing

Manages follow-up testing programs after a violation per SAP recommendations

Who Is Required to Join a Consortium?

→

Owner-Operators (Required)

If you drive a commercial motor vehicle under your own USDOT number and have no employees, you are both the employer and the employee. FMCSA regulations prohibit you from managing your own random testing program — you must join a consortium under 49 CFR Part 382.305.

→

Small Carriers (Strongly Recommended)

Carriers with 1–5 drivers typically use a C/TPA because maintaining a statistically valid random pool with so few drivers is impractical on your own. A consortium pools you with thousands of drivers so the math works correctly.

→

Larger Fleets (Optional)

Fleets with many drivers can self-administer, but many use a C/TPA to reduce administrative burden, ensure accurate random selection, and maintain proper MRO and Clearinghouse compliance.

How the Random Draw Works

1

Enroll & Enter the Pool

Once you enroll, your name enters the random selection pool. Every driver in the consortium has an equal probability of selection in each draw period.

2

Random Selection Draw

Using a random number generator, the C/TPA selects the required percentage of the pool each draw period (typically quarterly). Selections are completely random — past selections do not reduce your future probability.

3

You Are Notified

If selected, you receive notification from WorkOccMed. You must report to a collection site the same day (for pre-shift) or before your next duty period begins. The notification specifies the collection type and deadline.

4

Specimen Collection

You visit any authorized collection site. For drug tests, a urine specimen is collected, sealed, and sent to a SAMHSA-certified lab. For alcohol tests, a breath alcohol test is administered on-site.

5

MRO Review & Result

A Medical Review Officer reviews the lab result. Negative results are reported quickly. Non-negative results trigger donor interview procedures before any violation is reported.

6

Clearinghouse Reporting

All violations are reported to the FMCSA Clearinghouse within 3 business days. Negative results are not reported. Annual limited queries by prospective employers will show no record for compliant drivers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a C/TPA?

A Consortium/Third-Party Administrator manages your DOT random drug and alcohol testing program — maintaining the pool, conducting draws, notifying selected drivers, coordinating with the MRO, and reporting to the FMCSA Clearinghouse.

Do I need a C/TPA physically near me?

No. WorkOccMed handles all administrative services electronically. Testing is done at 15,000+ collection sites near you — the C/TPA just manages the paperwork and compliance from anywhere in the country.

Who is required to join a consortium?

Owner-operators are required by 49 CFR Part 382.305. Small carriers with limited drivers typically must also join a consortium to maintain a statistically valid random pool.

What are the 2026 FMCSA random testing rates?

50% for drug testing and 10% for alcohol testing of the driver pool per year. These are minimum rates — FMCSA can increase them if industry-wide positive rates rise above thresholds.

What happens if I'm selected and don't test?

Refusing to test is treated the same as a positive result under 49 CFR Part 382. You must stop performing safety-sensitive functions immediately and begin the return-to-duty process with a SAP evaluation.

Can I switch C/TPAs mid-year?

Yes. You can transfer your enrollment to a new C/TPA at any time. Your new C/TPA will collect your testing history and enroll you in their pool. Contact us and we'll coordinate the transfer.
