Who Needs a DOT Consortium?

Required for CDL Drivers
Subject to FMCSA

If you operate a commercial motor vehicle (CMV) in interstate commerce and hold a CDL, federal law requires consortium enrollment. This includes:

Owner-Operators

Independent CDL drivers operating under their own authority or leased to a carrier are required to be in a consortium. This is one of the most common compliance gaps.

Small Fleets (1�50 Drivers)

Small motor carriers who don't have enough drivers to meet their own random testing rate pool can satisfy FMCSA requirements by joining a third-party consortium.

Carriers Under 50 CMVs

Motor carriers with fewer than 50 CMVs in a random pool statistically cannot achieve the federally required random rate on their own � a consortium solves this.

FAQs

Common Questions

What is a C/TPA?

A Consortium/Third-Party Administrator (C/TPA) is an organization that manages drug and alcohol testing programs on behalf of DOT-regulated employers. We handle random selection, MRO coordination, recordkeeping, and FMCSA reporting.

What is the current FMCSA random testing rate?

For 2025, FMCSA requires a minimum random drug testing rate of 50% of the average number of driver positions and a minimum alcohol testing rate of 10%. These rates are set annually based on industry-wide positive test rates.

What happens if my driver tests positive?

A positive result reported by the MRO means the driver must immediately be removed from safety-sensitive duties and referred to a Substance Abuse Professional (SAP). The driver cannot return to CDL duties until completing the SAP evaluation and return-to-duty process.

How quickly can I enroll?

Same day. Complete your enrollment at portal.dot-physical.net and receive your consortium documentation within hours. You'll be added to the random pool immediately.

Do I need a consortium if I'm leased to a motor carrier?

If you're an owner-operator leased to a carrier, the carrier is typically responsible for your DOT drug and alcohol testing program. Confirm with your carrier whether you're enrolled in their program or if you need your own consortium enrollment.

What is an MIS report and do I need to file one?

A Management Information System (MIS) report is an annual FMCSA report summarizing your testing activity for the year. We file this on your behalf as part of your consortium membership � you don't need to do anything.

What is the difference between a consortium and a random pool?

A consortium (C/TPA) is the organization that administers your program. A random pool is the group of drivers from which random selections are made. When you join our consortium, you're added to our managed random pool automatically.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Annual consortium fees are non-refundable but you can cancel at the end of your membership period. We'll provide all your records in a portable format so you can transfer to another C/TPA if needed.
